Moderation & right of reply ⚖️

Fair to consumers. Fair to businesses. Transparent to everyone.

Our standards

  • Reports must describe the author’s own experience and stick to verifiable facts.
  • We remove names, addresses, phone numbers and photos of private individuals (e.g. employees).
  • Opinion must be clearly framed as opinion. Accusations of crimes require official sources.
  • We don’t publish threats, hate, or calls for harassment. Shame the practice, not the person.

Right of reply

Any business mentioned can submit a public response, displayed alongside the report. Use the contact form (topic: “Business right of reply”). We don’t charge for this — ever.

Corrections & takedowns

If content is inaccurate, send the URL and supporting evidence. We review within 5 business days and will correct, annotate or remove content that fails our standards. We will not remove accurate, lawful reports in exchange for payment.

Legal notices

Court orders and legal notices can be submitted through the contact form. We comply with applicable law in the jurisdictions where we operate.
