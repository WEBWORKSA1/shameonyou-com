Privacy & cookie policy

Short version: we don’t sell your data, and your contact details are never published.

Advertisement
Ad space · Google AdSense (header) · Advertise here

Last updated: September 30, 2026

What we collect

  • Information you submit via forms (name, email, optional phone, country, report details, evidence links).
  • Usage data via cookies and analytics (pages viewed, device, approximate location) if you consent.
  • Advertising data: Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites.

How we use it

  • To review, moderate and publish reports (never with your contact details).
  • To respond to you and provide free case reviews — including, with your consent, sharing your case with a relevant partner.
  • To send newsletters you subscribe to (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To run and improve the site, prevent spam and abuse, and measure performance.

Google AdSense & cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and/or other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Form processing

Forms are delivered to our team by a third-party form processor (FormSubmit) and payments are processed by PayPal or other listed platforms; we never see or store your card number.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, India DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to processing. Use our contact form. We respond within 30 days.

Retention & security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No method is 100% secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data.
