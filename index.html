FAQ

Questions, answered

Is ShameOnYou.com free?

Yes. Reporting, tools, guides and case reviews are free for consumers. We’re funded by ads, sponsors and reader donations.

Can I report anonymously?

Yes. Your name and contact details are never published. We only use them to follow up with you.

Do you name and shame individuals?

No. We focus on business practices and patterns. We don’t publish private individuals, personal addresses or unverified accusations. See our Moderation Policy.

I’m a business that was mentioned. Can I respond?

Absolutely. Every business gets a right of reply displayed alongside the report. Use the contact form.

Will you get my money back?

We can’t promise recovery, but our free case review routes you to the people most likely to help — card-dispute guidance, attorneys, and official agencies.
