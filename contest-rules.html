Official contest rules

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.
  2. Eligibility: entrants must be 18+ or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. Employees of ShameOnYou.com and sponsors are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions where such contests are prohibited are excluded.
  3. Entry period: as stated on each contest page; entries after the deadline are void.
  4. How to enter: submit the entry form with original content you own. One entry per person per contest unless stated.
  5. Judging: skill-based, by a panel, on accuracy/evidence (40%), public-interest value (30%), clarity/creativity (30%). Decisions are final.
  6. Prizes: as listed; paid by PayPal or bank transfer within 30 days of verification. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes. Canadian residents may be required to answer a skill-testing question.
  7. License: entrants grant ShameOnYou.com a non-exclusive license to display entries with credit.
  8. Privacy: entrant data is used only to administer the contest per our Privacy Policy.
  9. Sponsor: ShameOnYou.com. Contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any social media platform.
