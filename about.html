Our mission

ShameOnYou.com exists to make deceptive business practices expensive and embarrassing — and to make getting help easy. We turn individual bad experiences into public patterns that warn millions.

What we stand for

  • Practices, not people. We expose business behaviour, not private individuals.
  • Facts first. Human moderation, evidence standards and a right of reply.
  • Never pay-to-hide. No business can pay to remove a legitimate report.
  • Free for consumers. Funded by labeled ads, sponsors and readers.

Methodology

Every report is screened for personal data, defamatory statements and evidence. Patterns become Hall of Shame entries only after multiple independent reports or credible public sources. Shame Scores combine community votes, report volume, financial harm and ‘escape difficulty’.

Funding transparency

Revenue comes from advertising (Google AdSense and direct), sponsorships, referral partnerships (disclosed) and donations. Editorial decisions are independent of all revenue sources.
